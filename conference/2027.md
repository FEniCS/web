# Description

The FEniCS 2027 conference is an opportunity for all those interested in
the FEniCS Project and related projects to exchange ideas, communicate
their results and network with the automated scientific computing
community. It will be hosted at
[BAM - Bundesanstalt für Materialforschung und -prüfung](https://www.bam.de/)
in Berlin, Germany.

We welcome developers, existing and potential users of the
FEniCS ecosystem as well as mathematicians, computer scientists and
application domain specialists interested in numerical methods, their
implementation and applications.

The FEniCS 2027 conference will emphasize an open and inclusive
atmosphere, contributed talks from a diverse range of scientific areas,
and dedicated time for discussions and coding.

Links to previous FEniCS conferences can be found [here](index.md).

## Registration and accommodation

### Registration

All participants must register for the conference.

- Registration link will be posted in early 2027.
- The early bird registration fee is 310 €; will open in early 2027, and will stay open until **TBA**.
- The standard registration fee is 385 €; registration closes **TBA**.
- The registration fee includes conference activities, coffee breaks, lunches,
  the conference dinner, and social events.
- The registration fee is non-refundable.

### Accommodation

The FEniCS conference registration fee does not include accommodation.
Participants are responsible for arranging their own accommodation.

## Travel awards

The [Frank Hirschvogel Foundation](https://frankhirschvogelstiftung.de/en/home/) and [Flax & Teal](https://flaxandteal.co.uk/) have kindly offered to support travel awards for attendees who would otherwise struggle to attend the conference for financial reasons. The awards may cover all or part of the eligible travel expenses and will be paid as a reimbursement following successful participation in the conference. Priority will be given to junior students and researchers, and to participants working in countries with a less developed research infrastructure.

Applications must be sent to <a href="mailto:francesco.ballarin@unicatt.it">Prof. Francesco Ballarin</a> and must include:

- a CV;
- a tentative abstract of the proposed contribution, which will also need to be formally submitted once abstract submission opens;
- a short letter of motivation;
- information on whether a visa is required to travel to Germany and, if so, whether an official invitation letter is required, together with the expected visa processing time;
- an estimate of the total travel expenses;
- a brief funding plan indicating how the remaining travel expenses would be covered if the travel award were to reimburse only either 25%, 50%, or 75% of the estimated total cost. This information will help the organizers assess whether partial funding would still make it possible for the applicant to attend the conference.

Applications will be assessed by the local organizing committee. The deadline
for applications is **10 January 2027**.

## Code of conduct

We expect all attendees of FEniCS 2027 to follow the FEniCS Project
[code of conduct](../community/code-of-conduct.md). Instances of
abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported
by contacting the conference organizers or the [FEniCS Steering
Council](https://github.com/FEniCS/governance).

## Best Presentation and Poster Awards

The following awards will be presented at FEniCS 2027:

- Best FEniCS 2027 Presentation by a PhD Candidate.
- Best FEniCS 2027 Presentation by a Postdoctoral Researcher.
- Best FEniCS 2027 Poster.
- Nate Sime Award for Exceptional FEniCS 2027 Visualization.

We thank [Ridgway Scott Foundation](https://people.cs.uchicago.edu/~ridg/prizes/prizes.html) for supporting the
Best Presentations and Poster Awards, and Nate Sime for
supporting the Exceptional Visualization Award.

All eligible presentations and posters will be evaluated by the FEniCS 2027
Award Committee appointed by the FEniCS Steering Council.
